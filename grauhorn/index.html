01 — The mountain

Colour
is overrated.

Up here everything turns to white, ash and granite. No billboards on the pistes, no music on the lifts. Just 118 km of groomed corduroy, couloirs that hold powder for days, and a valley that goes quiet when the last chair stops.

02 — What we offer

Eight ways
down (and one up)

Everything you need for a day on the mountain sits within one pass and one valley. Hover a line to look closer.

01

Pistes

118 km groomed nightly by a fleet of 14 snowcats, from wide blue cruisers at the base to the 3.4 km Granite black off the summit.

All levels
02

The Foundry park

A 1.2 km snowpark with 40+ features, three jump lines, a 6 m superpipe and a beginner line with soft boxes. Reshaped every Thursday.

Freestyle
03

Freeride zones

Four patrolled off-piste sectors and two couloirs, avalanche-controlled each morning. Transceiver check gates at every entrance.

Advanced
04

Ride school

Ski and snowboard lessons for ages 4 and up. Small groups of max. six, private coaching, and a three-day "first turns" guarantee.

Lessons
05

Edge Lab rental

Current-season skis and boards, boot fitting with heat-moulded liners, and a workshop that sharpens and waxes overnight.

Gear
06

Guided touring

Splitboard and ski-touring days with certified mountain guides. Skins, probes and beacons included. The "one up" from the heading.

Backcountry
07

Night runs

Wednesday and Saturday, 18:30–22:00. Four floodlit pistes and the park's jump line, followed by fondue at the Kessel hut.

After dark
08

Summit lodge & sauna

Twenty-two rooms at 2,890 m, a wood-fired sauna on the ridge and a kitchen that cooks with what the valley grows.
